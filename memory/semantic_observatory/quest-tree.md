## [QST-001] Introduction

Knowledge systems often provide information.

Quest Trees provide direction.

A Quest Tree is a navigational structure built upon observations, cards, relationships, and vertical layers.

Its purpose is not to represent everything that exists.

Its purpose is to suggest meaningful journeys through what exists.

Quest Trees transform knowledge into exploration.

---

## [QST-002] Graphs and Paths

A graph and a path are not the same thing.

A graph describes possibilities.

A path describes a journey.

A graph may contain countless routes.

A Quest Tree proposes one route among many.

The purpose of the graph is completeness.

The purpose of the quest is guidance.

---

## [QST-003] Guidance

Knowledge can overwhelm.

A large collection of observations may contain hundreds or thousands of possible directions.

A Quest Tree reduces uncertainty by suggesting meaningful next steps.

Guidance is not control.

Guidance is the reduction of unnecessary complexity.

The quest points toward a destination while preserving freedom of exploration.

---

## [QST-004] Learning Paths

Quest Trees may represent learning journeys.

Examples:

Arithmetic
↓
Algebra
↓
Calculus

or

EOQ
↓
EPQ
↓
Backorder EOQ
↓
Discount EOQ

Each step builds upon previous observations.

The path provides structure for understanding.

---

## [QST-005] Quest Chains

Complex objectives may be divided into smaller quests.

A Quest Chain consists of multiple connected objectives.

For example:

Understand Requests
↓
Understand APIs
↓
Understand Distributed Systems

Individual quests provide progress.

Quest Chains provide direction.

---

## [QST-006] Exploration and Discovery

Not all exploration follows predefined routes.

Quest Trees should guide without eliminating discovery.

A good quest structure reveals opportunities rather than restricting them.

The purpose of a quest is not to replace curiosity.

The purpose of a quest is to support curiosity.

---

## [QST-007] Adaptive Quest Trees

Different explorers require different journeys.

A beginner and an expert may begin from different observations.

Quest Trees may therefore adapt to:

* prior knowledge
* goals
* interests
* context

Navigation should remain flexible while preserving coherence.

---

## [QST-008] Navigation and Compression

Quest Trees act as compression mechanisms.

A large graph may contain thousands of observations.

A quest highlights only a small subset.

500 observations
↓
50 candidates
↓
5 recommended paths
↓
1 next meaningful step

Navigation therefore becomes a form of semantic compression.

The objective is not simplification alone.

The objective is clarity.

---

## [QST-009] Principles

A graph shows what is possible.

A quest shows what is meaningful next.

Guidance should preserve freedom.

Navigation should reduce complexity.

Discovery should remain possible.

Understanding emerges through journey.
