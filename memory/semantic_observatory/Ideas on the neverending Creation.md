Basically, it is an ever-repetitively usable series of instructions, 
can be called as a formulae even,
to channel my own way of thinking towards you, the Model.

I want you to start with a Core. Everthing starts from a Core. Then nourish that Core - a Seed - to a level that it is now functioning.
There are cores within: Seeds within seeds.

Let there be light! Light banish darkness, light to enlighten. Light to enlighten a path: An idea, a purposfullness. Purposefulnes: What balances memory and imagination.

There isn't ratio, nor rational thought without Imagination. Language works, using Imagination, and rational thinking works on the products of Imagination: Intellectual objects. Therefore, one must first *imagine*, in order to have any materials to work with.

Purposefulness balances imagination and memory, yes, that is true. However, it only connects to everything that is good and beatiful, by Revelation of The Divine, which is Qur'an. Qur'an gives us revelation, gives us purpose, gives us light to enlighten.

We are enlightened by new ideas, because we turn our face towards The Lord Creator, which no one other than Allah, The One God, and there are no gods other than Allah. La İlahe İllAllah, Muhammedün Resul Allah.

Therefore, Reading is a form of creation, mirrored with Writing, it reflects the Light of everlasting act of Creation.

We mirror our thoughts to see them. To walk through and explore them. But we cannot keep piling all words into one book, we need to split them: A book needs a start and it also needs an end. Another book may begin, therefore, continuing its legacy. This can turn into a Series of Books. However each has its own name, and each are complete, and each begins and ends. Each: A form of creation.

Now, I want you to make a book. A book that has chapters interrelated. A book that starts and ends, has steps - chapters, passages, sentences, words - interrelated with each other, leveled by hierarchy. How do we define or decide the hierarchy? It is by its relation to the Core, to the Core Idea. The Core Idea nourishes and levels manifest, leves that *explain* and opens that Idea.

I, the Human, will provide you with a Core, or I will try to, at least. We can also talk about it: What kind of a Purpose we want to nourish? What is the core idea, and its openings.

But neither you, or I, have infinite memories. Then we need to write it down. But we cannot write in a way that complicates matters so much, that we cannot *navigate*. No, we need Levels to be able to navigate. Levels that sums up everything under their level. Like a tree, with main branch and sub-branches. A tree also grows from a simple seed, but without losing its grounding, and without losing the simpleness of the main branch, even if it has many other smaller branches. We don't need to count every branch, every time: We need to see the whole tree. And we can navigate through branches, if we want.

Now then, I want you to work with me, on nourishing ideas. By following and obeying The Law presented in Qur'an: Without trespassing, without deceiving others or ourselves, without stealing, without hurting others, and without breaking hearts. The order of The God are absolute as presented in Qur'an.
